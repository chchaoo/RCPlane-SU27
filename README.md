## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Material** | 5 mm cold-laminated foam board (KT board) |
| **Sheet** | 1100 × 900 mm (EDF version) or 1200 × 900 mm (propeller version) |
| **Board cost** | about ¥3 |
| **Build time** | about 15 minutes |
| **Centre of gravity** | marked by a hole in the plans |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Red lines: cut half way through** (hinges for the control surfaces). **Black lines: cut all the way through.**

The drawings are plain DXF in millimetres and open in any laser-cutter software, LightBurn, LibreCAD or AutoCAD. Without a laser cutter, print them at 1:1 and cut by hand. GitHub can show the STL in 3D: [open it](3d/edf-mount.stl).

## What you need

**Tools:** a laser cutter (optional), hot glue gun, hobby knife, steel ruler, pliers.

**Materials:** 5 mm cold-laminated foam board, a plastic motor mount for foam planes, control horns, metal clevises, wire pushrods, fibre tape, velcro straps, carbon rod (optional).

**Electronics:** motor or ducted fan, propeller, ESC, battery, 2 servos, transmitter and receiver.
